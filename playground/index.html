How it works. Your text is turned into phonemes by eSpeak NG 1.52 (compiled to WebAssembly, the same version used in training) with Sinhala-specific corrections, then a 4.5M-parameter tiny-voca model makes the audio with ONNX Runtime Web. Nothing is sent to a server.

Voices. Ven. Mettananda (M1) and Mrs. Oshadi (F1), from the Path Nirvana Sinhala TTS dataset (GPL-3.0). The speakers donated their voices for non-obscene, non-offensive speech generation only: please do not use this to impersonate them or for deceptive, obscene or offensive content.

Limits. Pronunciation follows eSpeak NG's Sinhala rules: digits, abbreviations and Latin-script words come out approximately, and the voices are trained on formal prose.

Model: eminduperera/tiny-voca-sinhala · Code: Emindu/tiny-voca-sinhala · Based on tiny-voca by Muhammed Nazeem.
